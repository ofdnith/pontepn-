## What Ponte is

Ponte copies files over your local network, from a folder on a Mac to a console you own. It is a personal, non-commercial project.

## What Ponte is not and does not include

- **No games**, and no pointers to where to get them. It only sees what you yourself put in the library folder.
- **No keys, firmware or system files** from any console.
- **It does not unlock consoles.** It contains no exploits and no way to circumvent protections. The PS5 and the Switch must already have homebrew enabled by third-party tools, which are not part of this repository.
- **No proprietary code, libraries or SDKs** from Sony or Nintendo. The PS5 payload is built with the community payload SDK and links no system library at build time; the Switch app uses libnx and the engine of [Sphaira](https://github.com/ITotalJustice/sphaira), both open source.

## Use

Only use Ponte with games, updates, DLC and other content you have the right to use: copies of games you own, homebrew and your own content. You are responsible for complying with the laws of your country and the terms of use of your consoles. Modifying a console may void its warranty and get it banned from online services.
